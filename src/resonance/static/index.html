How to film (30 seconds of care saves a re-shoot)
  1. Slow motion (240 fps). Send the original file: AirDrop, cable or "Files"; chat apps re-encode it at normal speed.
  2. Phone braced on a tripod, railing or wall, about 1–1.5 m from a bench rope or 3–10 m from a bridge cable. Do not hold it in the air.
  3. Film side-on, the cable across the frame against sky or a plain wall. Tap and hold on the cable to lock focus and exposure.
  4. Keep something that does not move in view (tower, wall, rock): it becomes the anchor that removes camera shake.
  5. Pluck the rope once (bench) or let wind and footfall excite the bridge, and film at least 5 s.
  6. For a confirmed result film a second take at a different rate (120 fps).
